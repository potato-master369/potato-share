Potato Share is Free and Open Source software. It aims to act as the ultimate cloud storage solution, like Google Drive, but free. Wouldn't it be nice to have your own little cloud? Together, we can make the cloud fast, fun, and free for everyone. Potato Share uses very little RAM on server, and can be light on client. Why would I say "can"? Potato Share is completely themable! That means you client can either be really fast, or really slow!

This is the Default+ theme, which builds on Default, the standard and lightweight theme, to make a better and more adaptive experience for you.
